# Source intake policy

## Allowed output

Record only verifiable language facts and metadata: lexical form, reading, concise sense label, grammar constraint, source licence, attribution, source SHA-256, retrieval time, or an official exam's public structure.

## Prohibited output

Do not record copied source passages, question stems, choices, answers, explanations, transcripts, audio, answer keys, scans, screenshots, or bulk corpus text. Do not turn official JLPT/TOPIK materials into a question bank.

## Required provenance

Every source needs its exact URL, licence ID and URL, attribution, allowed-use statement, retrieval time, and SHA-256. Missing or incompatible terms block intake. An intake artifact is evidence for independently written material, not permission to reproduce the source.

## Pronunciation

Use browser Google speech only. R2 pronunciation storage, generation, playback, activation, and fallback are prohibited. R2 report and evidence buckets are unrelated and must not be changed by this workflow.
